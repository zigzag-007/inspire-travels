About Inspire Travels

A Sri Lankan travel company built around real conversations, flexible routes, and the people who make this island feel like home.

Born from a Passion for Sri Lanka

Inspire Travels began in 2020 with Kalum Ranathunga and a simple belief: the best Sri Lanka trips should feel personal. We shape each route around the people travelling, then connect them with places, communities, and local guides we know.

We stay close from the first message to the final drop off. That means honest advice, flexible pacing, and thoughtful details that make a holiday feel effortless without making it feel packaged.

Inspiring Authentic Connections

Our mission is to be the unforgettable trip and creative Sri Lankan holiday specialist. We are dedicated to creating transformative travel experiences that go beyond ordinary tourism, offering authentic encounters with Sri Lanka's rich culture, stunning landscapes, and warm-hearted people. Every journey we craft is designed to leave an indelible mark on your heart and soul.

With over six years of expertise in the travel industry, we have perfected the art of storytelling through travel. We believe that every destination has a story to tell, and we are the passionate narrators who bring these stories to life. From the misty tea plantations to the golden beaches, from ancient temples to bustling markets, we ensure that your Sri Lankan adventure becomes a masterpiece of memories that will inspire you for years to come.
